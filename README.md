One line, 120 characters (cap 200): a roast of an alarm clock that
rings at 6 AM on a Saturday morning. Entry for the make.fun bounty
"Snooze Button Roast" (u-carbon-forge-snooze-button-roast-14cbafc6).

> My alarm went off at 6am on Saturday, so I snoozed it 11 times, woke up at 9:03, and was somehow still late for nothing.

## Why this line

- One filmable scene: the alarm, eleven snoozes, a 9:03 wake-up.
- The punch lands in the final clause: late for nothing, on a Saturday.
- Hyper-specific numbers (11 times, 9:03) instead of a flat premise.
- The joke grows from the snooze mechanism itself — nine-minute
  installments of sleep that leave you more tired, and somehow late.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Prints the line and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
